# Shadow + pet-pet scenes — production storyboard

Status: production contract, 2026-08-15. The room director owns position and
facing; authored frames own contact. Particles may accent an action but may
never be the action.

## Permanent Shadow actions

### 1. Call Shadow / Позвать Тень — P0 batch built

1. Traveller turns toward the Shadow and opens one gloved palm (0–0.7s).
2. The current Shadow form notices, leans, then flies to the hand on an arc
   with its own flight cycle (0.7–2.5s).
3. Atomic contact frame replaces Traveller + Shadow only: eye contact and palm
   touch are unambiguous (2.5–6.6s).
4. Live actors return with their own locomotion (6.6–8.4s).

Frames: the approved `attune-{spark,spirit,guardian,keeper}.png`. No evolution
fallback. The other residents remain present. The first greeting of a day may
use the existing bond transaction; animation repeats never grant a reward.

### 2. Talk / Поговорить — Shadow P0 next

1. Shadow moves to shoulder height and enters `listening`.
2. User speaks or types; the listening frame persists without a fake timer.
3. Shadow changes to an authored thinking frame while the answer is prepared.
4. Piper playback drives `speaking` pulses; the modal remains a real two-way
   conversation instead of replaying one authored sentence.

`Прислушаться` and `Подумать вместе` are internal phases, never buttons.

### 3. Check course / Проверить курс — Shadow P0 next

1. Traveller unfolds one map/plan prop.
2. Shadow moves above it and illuminates one point, not the whole map.
3. Traveller marks the point; the UI reveals exactly one next action.
4. The map closes before both actors return.

Two authored contact frames plus one common map prop. Copy rule: one concrete
next step; at most one clarifying question when selection is impossible.

## Contextual Shadow scenes

- Return: Shadow crosses the room to meet the returning user; no reproach copy.
- Breather: at low energy, Traveller sits and Shadow lowers to the same visual
  level; no task is created.
- Evening at the window: both face the window; restrained flame/breath loop.
- Rare victory: one physical shared gesture tied to a real achievement; no
  confetti loop and no reward for replay.

All four evolutions share timing/dramaturgy but keep their own silhouettes.

## Pet-pet matrix

### Gamabunta × Katsuyu — P0 batch built

After training. Gamabunta hops and Katsuyu crawls toward the free west meeting
zone. Atomic A: seated leg stretch, Katsuyu supports his forearm. Atomic B:
Gamabunta raises the free shoulder and Katsuyu extends to support the movement.
Both return visibly. Traveller, Shadow and Mister P remain in the room.

### Gamabunta × Mister P

Gamabunta begins repetitions. Mister P opens the journal, marks a count on
each downbeat, checks the total, closes it. Three frames: down / mark / final
count. State bias: BODY focus.

### Katsuyu × Mister P

Katsuyu settles after load. Mister P brings a cushion or light cover, adjusts
one edge, then steps back to verify comfort. Three frames. State bias: recovery
or return after absence.

### Shadow × Gamabunta

Shadow becomes a moving reaction target. Gamabunta blocks left/right without
striking it; Shadow stops and both acknowledge the clean sequence. Four frames.
State bias: BODY focus with normal energy.

### Shadow × Katsuyu

Katsuyu slows her breathing. Shadow lowers beside her; both flame and body
motion converge into one calm rhythm. Three frames. State bias: low energy.

### Shadow × Mister P

Mister P opens the ledger in dim light. Shadow becomes a steady lamp; Mister P
counts, marks one line and closes the book. Three frames. State bias: MONEY /
RESOURCES focus.

## Runtime gates

- Start only after Den Life has completed its first four-resident fairness lap.
- Never before 45 seconds after entering the Den.
- Maximum one pet-pet scene per visit.
- Shared post-scene cooldown: random 8–12 minutes.
- Selection is state scored; unavailable art is ineligible, never substituted.
- Reduced motion disables autonomous pet-pet scenes.
- No viewing/replay reward and no permanent pet-pet controls.
