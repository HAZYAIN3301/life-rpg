# Generation prompts — Katsuya Life v2

Все изображения генерировались как square production sprites на сплошной `#ff00ff` хроме. Общий lock: утверждённая кремово-белая Кацую с бирюзовыми листовыми слоями, тремя светлыми листовыми аппликациями, золотыми кончиками усиков и фактурой многослойной вырезанной бумаги. Для pair-кадров дополнительно фиксировался утверждённый худой мужской Traveller без рюкзака, сумки, фонаря и оружия.

## Solo

### glide-compress

`Locomotion keyframe A: body shorter and wider, lower body compressed into soft accordion folds, front only slightly raised, antennae angled forward; physical soft-body compression, same mass and identity, whole grounded sprite.`

### glide-extend

`Matching keyframe B: front third reaches gently forward, lower foot elongated along floor, rear body slightly narrower, belly folds opened; plausible slug glide deformation, same character and scale.`

### stretch-up

`Broad base planted; front half stretches upward in a graceful S curve; body remains thick and weighty, antennae high, belly folds opened, calm relieved closed-eye smile.`

### cushion-sleep

`Low broad sleeping curl on a correctly sized muted-teal oval cushion with thin warm-gold piping; relaxed antennae, coherent grounded silhouette, no levitation.`

### helpers

`Large guardian in a lowered nurturing pose with exactly two miniature cream-and-teal helper slugs on the same floor line; distinct bodies, one antenna curved protectively.`

## Pair

### greet-contact

`Traveller kneels naturally on one knee and places one open gloved palm against Katsuya; she leans into the touch and bends one antenna toward his hand; coherent contact anatomy and stable proportions.`

### breathe-in / breathe-out

`Cross-legged Traveller and grounded Katsuya perform a guided breathing loop: inhale has open chest, raised shoulders, upward palms and taller slug; exhale lowers shoulders, softens torso, relaxes palms and compresses slug. One subtle pale-teal paper halo arc.`

### restore-contact

`Tired but safe Traveller sits and leans upper back and shoulder against Katsuya; her three pale leaf appliques glow and a few close leaf motes rise. Quiet recovery, not an explosion.`

### stretch-a / stretch-b

`Stable seated Traveller and grounded Katsuya perform opposite held side stretches; clean anatomy, no overlap, no scale or camera change. Two slow alternating keyframes.`

Полные исходные хрома-кадры сохранены в `sources/`; production alpha — в `outputs/` и runtime-папках.
