# Kling prompts — Den v5 ambient loops

Use `masters/den-night-unlit.png` as the first-frame/reference image for all
night variants. Keep the camera perfectly locked. Export 5–8 seconds, 16:9,
seamless loop when available.

## Fireplace

Animate only the logs inside the fireplace: a natural small wood fire catches,
flames curl irregularly around the logs, embers glow and fade, and soft amber
light flickers physically across the nearby stone, mantel, map edge and floor.
Everything else remains perfectly still. No camera motion, no geometry changes,
no extra objects, no smoke filling the room, no cartoon blobs, no morphing.

## Rain outside

Natural rain falls only outside the arched window, behind the glass and exterior
tree branch. Thin droplets and occasional trails form on the outside surface of
the panes; the branch and distant valley react subtly to wind. The interior stays
completely dry and still. No rain in front of the window frame, no camera motion,
no lightning, no object changes, no glass deformation.

## Robin arrives and sings

A small detailed cut-paper robin flies in from outside, lands on the exterior
tree branch behind the glass, settles its wings, tilts its head and sings softly.
The branch flexes slightly under its weight. Keep the room and camera locked.
The bird must remain outside; never cross the glass or float in the room. No
additional birds, no morphing, no text. Optional subtle golden musical-note
paper motes may appear outside near the beak and dissolve.

## Daylight base

Keep the exact architecture, furniture and camera. Change only the exterior and
physically motivated illumination: clear daytime valley outside the window,
soft cool daylight entering through the panes, warmer restrained interior
bounce, fireplace remains unlit. No moved furniture, no new objects, no people,
no camera motion.
