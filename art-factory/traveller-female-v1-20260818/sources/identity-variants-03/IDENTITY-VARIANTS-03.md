# Traveller female identity variants 03 — canonical-style review

Status: **approval-only**. These images are keyed identity concepts, not normalized runtime art. Nothing in this directory may be copied to `public/art` before Albert selects one identity and the normal approval/factory QA cycle is repeated.

## Fixed contract

- `traveller1.png` is the authority for body proportions, head scale, eye grammar, outfit geometry, paper construction and silhouette.
- Eyes are two isolated solid-black paper ovals on the skin-colour face: no sclera, iris, pupil, highlight, lash or enclosing anime eye shape.
- The backpack, bedroll, lantern, shoulder strap and pouches are deliberately absent.
- Costume and palette remain canonical: brass goggles, rust scarf, teal/ochre coat, cream shirt, charcoal trousers, brown gloves and boots.
- All three variants keep the same neutral full-body runtime stance and differ mainly in hair silhouette.

## Candidates

1. `candidate-e1-layered-pixie-bob-keyed.png` — short layered pixie-bob with a confident side sweep.
2. `candidate-e2-asymmetric-bob-keyed.png` — clean asymmetric jaw-length bob, one side tucked behind the ear.
3. `candidate-e3-shaggy-bob-keyed.png` — softer shaggy bob with a few broad angular paper locks.

An earlier chin-bob generation was rejected before inclusion because it introduced a pale enclosing eye shape and eyelash language. It is intentionally absent from this batch.
